luka
Features Performance Privacy

The Minecraft 1.8.9 and 1.7.10 client built for PvP.

Fast and ready for big fights. Pick 1.8.9 or 1.7.10, Normal or Vulkan, on Windows or Linux.

Download for Windows Download for Linux

Version 1.5.1. Free and open source: source code under the GNU GPL v3, the Vulkan renderer under the GNU AGPL v3.

Everything in one client

1.8.9 and 1.7.10

Both versions from one launcher, with the same mods and menus. Pick the version next to the renderer.

Two renderers

Play on Normal or Vulkan. Switch between them from the launcher.

Windows and Linux

The launcher and the Vulkan renderer run on both. On Linux, unpack the download and run luka.sh.

Made for big fights

Players and items hidden behind walls are skipped, so crowded fights stay smooth.

Your HUD, your way

Drag, resize and style every HUD module. Start clean with just FPS, CPS and the scoreboard.

Replays

Record your fights, fly around them with a free camera, and render video with real motion blur.

Better chat

Smooth messages, no background if you want, and a sound when someone says your name.

Friends

Add friends, see who is online and join their server. Other luka players get a purple luka icon beside their name.

Updates itself

Every update is signed and arrives the next time you press Play.

Windows says "unknown publisher"?

luka isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may warn you. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Is it safe?

luka has no analytics or tracking, and every update is signed so only luka's own files get installed. See exactly what luka connects to.

Luka Online

Friends and the luka icon use luka's own service, api.luka.wtf. It gets your Minecraft name, that you are online, and (if you allow it) the server you are on, for your friends. Your sign-in token goes to Mojang only. Switch off the Luka Online card in the luka menu to turn it off. Details.
